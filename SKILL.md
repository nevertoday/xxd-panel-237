## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a neo-Y2K kawaii sticker zine collage. Turn the same subject into several die-cut fragments: different poses, scales and crops, one or two exaggerated close-ups, and a few intentionally imperfect sticker scraps. Each cutout has a clear white die-cut border. Scatter them with slight rotation and overlap, but keep a readable hierarchy. Pale gray-lavender ground, acid lime and cobalt as accents, plus a few colours taken from the source. Small handwritten micro-captions may feel like magazine notes or chat asides. Cute, weird, cheeky and youthful, but art-directed. It is not a cheap template, a childish cartoon, a tidy grid, a vintage brown collage or a literal photo dump.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-237/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
